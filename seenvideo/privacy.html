Privacy Policy

App: SeenVideo
Effective date: September 12, 2026
Last updated: September 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Sander Baas ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the SeenVideo Android application (the "App").

The App helps users keep track of the watch status of YouTube videos, such as whether a video has been watched, partially watched, or not watched.

By using the App, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy. Where required by law, we will ask for your consent before processing your personal information.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Watch-status information

Depending on how you use the App, we may process the following information:

  • YouTube video IDs and URLs;
  • video titles, thumbnails, channel names, or other publicly available video information;
  • your watch status, such as watched, unwatched, or partially watched;
  • your playback position or progress, if this feature is enabled;
  • the date and time when a video was added, watched, or updated;
  • optional notes, tags, folders, or ratings that you create in the App.

1.2 Account information

If the App supports user accounts or cloud synchronization, we may collect:

  • your email address or account identifier;
  • authentication tokens required to connect the App to your account;
  • your preferences and synchronization settings.

We do not ask you to provide your YouTube password. Authentication, if available, is handled through the relevant provider's authorization process.

1.3 Device and technical information

We may automatically receive limited technical information necessary to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the App, such as:

  • Android version and device type;
  • App version;
  • language and general regional settings;
  • diagnostic information and crash reports.

2. How We Use Information

We use information for the following purposes:

  • to provide and maintain the App;
  • to store and display your YouTube watch status;
  • to synchronize your watch status across your devices, if enabled;
  • to remember your settings and preferences;
  • to diagnose errors and improve performance;
  • to prevent fraud, abuse, and unauthorized access;
  • to comply with applicable legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your watch-status information for targeted advertising.

3. Legal Basis for Processing

Where data protection law applies, we process information based on one or more of the following legal grounds:

  • Performance of a service: processing is necessary to provide the App's core features.
  • Consent: where you choose to enable optional features such as account synchronization, analytics, or notifications.
  • Legitimate interests: to secure, maintain, and improve the App, provided that these interests do not override your rights.
  • Legal obligations: where processing is required by applicable law.

4. Local Storage and Cloud Synchronization

  • Local-only option: Watch-status information is stored only on your Android device and is not uploaded to our servers.

You can disable synchronization at any time in the App's settings. Disabling synchronization does not automatically delete previously synchronized information unless you select the deletion option.

5. YouTube API Services

The App may use YouTube API Services to retrieve information about YouTube videos or to provide YouTube-related functionality.

The use of YouTube API Services is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and Google's Privacy Policy .

We only request access to information needed to provide the App's features. We do not request or store your YouTube password.

If you authorize the App to access your Google or YouTube account, you can revoke that access through Google security settings or through the App's account settings.

After authorization is revoked, we will delete YouTube-related authorized data stored by us within 30 days, unless we are legally required to retain it.

6. Sharing of Information

We may share limited information with service providers that process data on our behalf, such as:

  • hosting and cloud-storage providers;
  • authentication and synchronization providers;
  • crash-reporting and diagnostic providers;
  • customer-support providers.

These providers may only process information as necessary to provide their services to us and must protect it appropriately.

We may also disclose information if required by law, court order, or a valid request from a competent authority, or when necessary to protect our rights, users, or the security of the App.

We do not sell, rent, or share your watch-status information with advertisers or data brokers.

7. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as necessary to provide the App, fulfill the purposes described in this Policy, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and comply with legal obligations.

Watch-status information stored in your account is retained until you delete it, delete your account, or request its deletion. Local data can usually be removed by deleting the relevant items in the App or uninstalling the App.

8. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure. These measures may include access controls, encryption in transit, and restricted access to production systems.

No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. Therefore, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • request access to the personal information we hold about you;
  • request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information;
  • request deletion of your information;
  • request restriction of certain processing;
  • object to certain processing;
  • request a portable copy of your information;
  • withdraw consent where processing is based on consent;
  • lodge a complaint with a data protection authority.

To exercise these rights, contact us using the details in the Contact Us section.

10. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 16, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children below that age.

If you believe that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us. We will take reasonable steps to delete that information.

11. International Data Transfers

Your information may be processed or stored in countries other than the country where you live. Where required by applicable law, we use appropriate safeguards for international transfers of personal information.

12. Third-Party Services and Links

The App may contain links to or integrate with third-party services, including YouTube and Google services. Those services have their own terms and privacy policies. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party services that we do not control.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we will update the “Last updated” date and, where appropriate, provide additional notice through the App or another reasonable method.
